Frequently Asked Questions

Is the middleman service really free?

Yes — 100% free. We don't take a cut of your trade, and there are no hidden charges. The only money that changes hands is whatever you agree to pay for the items themselves.

How do I know you're a real middleman?

Our verified accounts are pinned in the Discord server. Always confirm the username and ID in the server itself before sending anything — anyone messaging you first from a random account is an impersonator.

What can you middleman?

Limiteds, collectibles, accounts, Robux, gift cards, and most other Roblox-related trades. If you're unsure, just ask in the server before opening a ticket.

Who goes first in a trade?

Neither side trades directly with the other. Both parties send to the middleman account, and items are released once everything is confirmed.

What are your active hours?

We are online 24/7, Monday through Sunday. Open a ticket any time — day or night — and we'll get to it as soon as possible.
