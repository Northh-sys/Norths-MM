Buy Limiteds

Hand-picked limiteds, priced around current RAP and market value. Secure yours through our escrow.

What is RAP? RAP stands for Recent Average Price — the rolling average of what a limited has actually sold for recently on the Roblox resale market. It's the standard benchmark traders use to judge value.

How are prices set? Our USD prices are calculated from current RAP at the standard conversion rate. Because the limited market moves daily, the exact price is always confirmed in your ticket before you pay.

How do I buy? Switch to the Stock tab, pick your item, and tap "Buy on Discord". We'll handle the transfer through our secure escrow process.
