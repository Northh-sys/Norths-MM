Limiteds

Buy limiteds from us.

Hand-picked limiteds, priced around current RAP and market value. Click any item to open a ticket and secure it — first come, first served.

What is RAP? RAP stands for Recent Average Price — the rolling average of what a limited has actually sold for recently on the Roblox resale market. It's the standard benchmark traders use to judge value, but it's not always the same as the cheapest current listing.

How are prices set? Our USD prices are calculated from current RAP at the standard conversion rate. Because the limited market moves daily, the exact price is always confirmed in your ticket before you pay — the numbers here are a live estimate, not a locked quote.

How do I buy? Tap "Buy on Discord" on any item in the Stock tab. Open a ticket, confirm the item and price, and we'll handle the transfer through the same secure escrow process we use for every trade.

Don't see what you want? Ask in the server. We can source most classic limiteds on request, even if they're not listed here.
