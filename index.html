FAQ

Common questions.

How do I know you're a real middleman?

Our verified accounts are pinned in the Discord server. Always confirm the username and ID in the server itself before sending anything — anyone messaging you first from a random account is an impersonator. We will never DM you out of nowhere asking for items.

What can you middleman?

Limiteds, collectibles, accounts, Robux, gift cards, and most other Roblox-related trades. If you're unsure whether your trade qualifies, just ask in the server before opening a ticket.

Who goes first?

Neither side trades directly with the other. Both parties send to the middleman account, and items are released once everything is confirmed. That's the whole point of using us.

How long does a trade take?

Most trades wrap up in 10–20 minutes once both parties are online and ready. Larger or multi-item trades can take longer, and we'll tell you upfront if that's the case.

What happens if someone tries to back out?

If a trade is cancelled before completion, all held items go straight back to their original owners. Nothing is released early, so there's no way for one side to get stuck holding the bag.

What are your active hours?

We're usually online daily from around 3 PM to 1 AM GMT, and often later. Open a ticket any time — if we're offline, we'll get to it as soon as we're back.
